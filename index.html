About

I started at Warmy as a content creator and ended up running its webinars, The Ignition Podcast and its social strategy, before leading marketing.

Along the way I also ran the YouTube content for AnyBiz. Today I work independently with SaaS companies, AI platforms and builders who sell worldwide, and with local brands that have a story to tell. One person covers the whole path, from the first idea to the final export.

Services

Seven things I do myself, so nothing gets lost between the idea and the final cut.

  • Shoot planning

    Shot lists, locations, gear and schedule, decided before the camera comes out.

  • Scriptwriting

    Hooks, structure and lines written for how people really watch.

  • Shooting

    Interviews, events, talking heads and b-roll, in studio or on location.

  • Editing & post

    Pacing, sound, captions and motion, cut for each platform.

  • AI video

    Ads and series built with generative tools when a camera can’t get the shot.

  • Hosting

    Podcasts and live webinars, in front of the camera as well as behind it.

  • Content strategy

    What to make, for whom and where it goes, tied to business goals.
